# Google product coverage

PR5 establishes complete inventory and explicit disposition for the 68-product contract. `raw_preserved` means the immutable Takeout member is accounted for but no product-semantic parser is claimed. A bounded JSON/CSV/XML member may be ingested by the lossless generic fallback as `partially_structured`; that member-level result does not upgrade the product semantic status. Formats are not called observed until validated against a real export.
