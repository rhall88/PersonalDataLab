# Google product coverage

PR5 establishes complete inventory and explicit disposition for the 68-product contract. PR6 adds the high-value personal-timeline adapters and migration 007 relationship history. PR7 adds bounded communications ingestion. PR8 adds the rich-asset graph and bounded document extraction. PR9 adds AI, knowledge, and notes semantics. PR10 adds account, commerce, device, and home semantics. `raw_preserved` means the immutable Takeout member is accounted for but no product-semantic parser is claimed. A bounded JSON/CSV/XML member may be ingested by the lossless generic fallback as `partially_structured`; that member-level result does not upgrade the product semantic status. Formats are not called observed until validated against a real export.
